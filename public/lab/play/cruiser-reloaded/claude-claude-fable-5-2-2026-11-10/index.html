Field notes

Fuel drains all the time. Skim rocks and shots to siphon more.

A hit halves your multiplier and costs one gun cell.

Wipe a whole drone squad to earn a power cell.

Shoot mines from range. Their blast hurts drones.

The hot lane pays x1.5.
